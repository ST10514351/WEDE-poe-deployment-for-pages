About New Beginnings Foundation

Homelessness is a circumstance, not an identity

New Beginnings Foundation helps people move from the streets towards a better life through support with shelter, food, recovery and employment opportunities.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity and the chance to start again.

Our beginning

The story of our founder

The founder of New Beginnings Foundation is Thabo Mkwanazi.

Throughout his life, Thabo faced difficult circumstances that eventually left him homeless. He spent over a decade living on the streets of Johannesburg whilst struggling with addiction and the will to survive.

During this period, he felt that he had no family, purpose or future. However, he eventually decided that his circumstances did not have to determine the rest of his life.

Thabo began using places of worship and donation centres to access showers, clean clothing and grooming supplies.

Regaining his confidence allowed him to begin taking on small jobs. Through hard work and saving, he eventually earned enough money to move into his own home.

From survival to purpose

A new Beginning

Thabo used his new motivation and experience to start a landscaping company.

Through dedication the business became successful, and was eventually sold. This gave Thabo the freedom he had long strived for.

Instead of focusing only on his own success, Thabo decided to use his experience and finances to help people facing the same circumstances he once faced.

This decision led to the creation of New Beginnings Foundation.

Our mission

Giving people a second chance

New Beginnings Foundation exists to give people a second chance at life.

The organisation believes that every person deserves access to safe housing and the opportunity to rebuild their future.

Many people experiencing homelessness did not arrive there by choice. Financial pressure, addiction, unemployment and difficult personal circumstances can quickly change someone's life.
