Homlessness is a circumstance, not an identity

New Beginnings Foundation helps people move from the streets onto a better life with shelter, food and sobriety

Story of our Founder

The founder of this initiative is Thabo Mkwanazi. Throughout his life he has been neglected and often left to his own troubles, and like most people they piled up to a point where he had no choice but to face them. For Thabo, those choices left him homeless. He has spent over 10 years living on the streets of Johannesburg, stealing and salvaging what he can to feed into his drug addiction. Like most in this situation he believed that he was destined to die with no family or purpose; but deep-down Thabo knew that despite his bad choices, no human should ever have to suffer a reality like this, not with opportunity as abundant as it is. To prove it to himself, he made the decision to change. He showered at places of worship and visited donation hubs to receive clean clothes and grooming supplies. With this he gained the confidence to start odd jobs, and through countless hours of hard work and saving he managed to pull himself out and move into his first property. He used this newfound motivation and experience to start a landscaping company, which he later sold to a larger conglomerate, guaranteeing financial freedom for him and his future family. Instead of spending this on himself Thabo decided to help the people who are still in the same situation he was in, leading to the founding of the New Beginnings Foundation.

More on our mission

This foundation exists to give people a second chance at life. This organisation fundamentally believes in the right for every person to have housing, and in a worsening economy many of the people who find themselves on the streets were not there by choice; many people today are a few small mistakes away from that same fate.

Why help us?

The mission of this NPO is to help as many homeless people as possible clean themselves up and find employment; his goal is to give the talented people who have lost motivation a second chance.
