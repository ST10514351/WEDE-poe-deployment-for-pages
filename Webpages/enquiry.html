How you can help

What you can enquire about

Volunteer

Give your time and skills to our outreach, rehabilitation and job-readiness programmes. Volunteering is free and training is provided.

Sponsor a Person

Cover the monthly cost of one person's housing, rehabilitation or employment support from their first day until they are job-ready.

Sponsor a Placement

Fund a learnership or job placement so that someone leaving our programme has a guaranteed opportunity waiting for them.
