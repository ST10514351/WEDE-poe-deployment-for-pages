How your donation is handled

1. Payment

A secure contribution is made to the foundation.

2. Reference

The donation is linked to a unique reference for record keeping and transparency.

3. Allocation

Funds are allocated towards the foundation's programmes and the donor's selected priority.

4. Reporting

The foundation aims to provide regular updates showing how contributions are being used.
